# Power Tracks Glossary

## Core Concepts

### <a id="burst"></a>Burst
A sudden, high-energy price movement. The raw material for the Power Tracks engine.
*   *See also*: [Power Track](#power-track)

### <a id="power-track"></a>Power Track
A specific type of [Burst](#burst) that matches our algorithmic signature. It is characterized by a "K-Spike" event sequence and specific structural properties.

### <a id="gamma-magnet"></a>Gamma Magnet
A price level where dealers have significant options positions (high Gamma). These levels act as attractors or repellors for price action.

### <a id="cluster"></a>Cluster
A group of bursts that share similar structural characteristics.
-   **Cluster 1**: Aggressive, high-velocity moves.
-   **Cluster 3**: Sustained, high-volume moves.
-   **Cluster 0**: "Trap" or noise bursts.

## Technical Terms

### <a id="frame"></a>Frame
The compressed binary format used by the engine to store tick data. Includes a CRC checksum for integrity.

### <a id="k-spike"></a>K-Spike
A discrete event signature derived from the raw price path. It simplifies the complex waveform into a sequence of key events (e.g., "Sharp Rise" -> "Plateau" -> "Dip").

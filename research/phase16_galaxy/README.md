# Phase16 Galaxy

## Brief

## Objective
To map the "Contagion Surface" of the May 2024 Meme Event across ~180 tickers spanning all major sectors (Tech, Finance, Consumer, Industrial, Meme).
We compared **War Week** (May 13-17) vs **Peace Week** (Apr 15-19) to calculate the **"War Diff"** (Activation magnitude).

## Key Findings

## Conclusion ("The Verdict")
The "War" is a **Contained Event**. It operates on a distinct "Frequency" (Opcode dialect) that affects a specific whitelist of tickers (KOSS, GME, CLOV, OPEN). It does **not** leak into the broader market infrastructure (SPY, AAPL).

The "War Algo" is likely a single, coordinated entity trading a predefined basket of ~10-20 "Deep Value / Short Squeeze" assets.
